About TechKrunch

TechKrunch is our premier technical festival designed to push the boundaries of innovation and creativity. It serves as a hub for technical knowledge, gaming excellence, and student participation, fostering a spirit of healthy competition.

This year, PRAGYOTSAV 2K26 is proudly organized under the banner of TechKrunch. Together, they create an immersive experience that bridges the gap between engineering fundamentals and futuristic technology.
